# The 17 prompts (full text)

Source: AI-Powered Job Application Playbook, Sasank Namburi. Run in order, in one session.

## SECTION 01 JD ANALYSIS

### Prompt 1: Load the Materials
I'm going to give you three documents to analyse in this session. Please read them carefully before doing anything.

1. My resume (pasted below)
2. My LinkedIn profile (below)
3. The job description (below)

Confirm you have read all three and are ready before proceeding.

[PASTE RESUME]
[PASTE LINKEDIN OR URL]
[PASTE JD]

### Prompt 2: Trigger Intelligence (web search required)
SEARCH AND INVESTIGATE:
1. Company + recent news (last 12 months): funding, acquisitions, leadership, launches
2. Company + role title + hiring patterns
3. Hiring manager background, posts, what they've built
4. Company strategic priorities: annual reports, CEO interviews
5. Programs/platforms mentioned in JD: stage, stakes

OUTPUT: TRIGGER INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
EXTERNAL TRIGGERS: confirmed + INFERRED signals
INTERNAL TRIGGERS: growth, restructure, new leadership
ROLE ORIGIN HYPOTHESIS: why now? 2-3 sentences.
COST OF NOT FILLING: what breaks in 3 months?

Be specific. Name companies, programs, dates. Flag every assumption.
Do not skip Prompt 2. It shapes everything that follows.

### Prompt 3: HM Attraction Profile
INSTANT PATTERN MATCH
- Which specific company logos make them lean forward? Not industry. Specific logos.
- Which program/platform names create instant recognition?
- Which technology names: specific, not generic?
- Which domain/business context signals right environment?

CREDIBILITY SIGNALS
- Team size, budget scale that matches their environment
- Stakeholder level: C-suite, board, regulators, enterprise
- Type of complexity navigated

UNCONSCIOUS DISQUALIFIERS
- Experience too small, unrelated, or junior
- Gaps that create hesitation

Present as structured profile. Name companies specifically. No generic answers.

### Prompt 4: The Real Ask
Choose the frame that fits:
- Something broke: system, team, process, delivery
- Something is being built: new capability/product/market
- Something is changing: leadership, strategy, scale
- Something was lost: key person, institutional knowledge

Then answer:
1. What must this hire get right in the first 90 days?
2. What does failure look like at 12 months?
3. What would make the HM think: "that is exactly the problem we have"?

Answer in plain language. Short sentences. No corporate framing. Think like the hiring manager, not HR.

## SECTION 02 FITMENT REPORT

### Prompt 5: Fitment Analysis
For each of 3-5 critical capabilities:
- Where it shows in my background (quote the role/achievement)
- Match: Strong / Partial / Gap
- Strongest proof point

Present as a table: Capability | Evidence | Strength

### Prompt 6: Fitment Report
OVERALL FIT SCORE: X/10 (with 2-sentence rationale)

TOP 3 STRENGTHS:
- [strength] - [evidence]

GAPS TO ADDRESS:
- [gap] - [how to minimise]

WINNING ANGLE: 2-3 sentences. Specific. No fluff. Speak like a trusted advisor.

### Prompt 7: My Unique Advantage
Looking at my background against this role, what is my Mountain of Value?

Meaning: what is the accumulated weight of experience, impact, and credibility that makes me uniquely valuable, not just qualified, but hard to replace once they meet me?

Answer in 3-4 sentences. This will be the core of everything else we write.

FORMAT:
Having [done X] and [done Y] at [A] and [B], I believe I will be a strong fit for this [role] at [company].
[Proof point 1] · [Proof point 2] · [Proof point 3]
Mountain of Value: not just qualified, but hard to replace.

## SECTION 03 COVER LETTER

### Prompt 8: Cover Letter Draft
STRUCTURE (follow exactly):
S1: "Having [X] and [Y] at [A] and [B], I'll be a strong fit for [role] at [company]."
S2-4: Three proof points. Outcomes, not duties. No adjectives.
S5: Additional value not in your headline.
Final: "Looking forward to exploring [initiative]."
Close: Regards, [Name]

RULES:
- No: "passionate", "dynamic", "proven track record"
- Short sentences only
- No more than 3/4 of a page
- Direct, tech-professional, no fluff

### Prompt 9: Formatting Pass
Font: Arial/Calibri 10.5-11pt · Spacing: 1.15 · Margins: 2.5cm+ · Length: 3/4 page max

### Prompt 10: Alignment Reprompt
Read the cover letter next to the JD analysis. Find alignment gaps: places where the letter doesn't directly speak to the CORE PROBLEM the company is solving.

Rewrite only those sentences. Don't change what's working. Don't add length. Don't add numbers unless they change meaning.

Goal: HM reads this and thinks: "this person gets what we need."

### Prompt 11: Final Read Check
Check for:
- AI-sounding phrases (flag + suggest replacements)
- Sentences over 20 words (cut or split)
- Vague claims with no grounding
- Adjectives without evidence

Return: changes list + final clean version.
No adjectives without evidence. Short sentences. In your voice.

## SECTION 04 RESUME

### Prompt 12: Resume Title
Rewrite my resume title to match this role.

Rules:
- Match or mirror the target role title
- 4-8 words maximum
- No adjectives: Senior, Dynamic, Passionate
- Immediately recognisable to a recruiter

Give me 3 options. I'll choose.

### Prompt 13: Executive Summary
Rules:
- 2-4 sentences maximum
- Reads like a senior professional
- No numbers in the summary
- Reference scope, domain, impact type
- No adjectives without evidence

First-person · present tense · active voice

### Prompt 14: Core Capabilities Section
Write my Core Capabilities section based on the JD.

This is a short list of 6-10 skill areas: not bullet points of duties, not soft skills. These are capability domains I operate in.

Format: Two or three columns, clean labels only.

If the role has a significant tech stack: add a "Key Technologies" line directly beneath Core Capabilities. NOT at the bottom of the resume. List only technologies evidenced on my resume.

RESUME ORDER: 1 Name + contact  2 Title  3 Exec summary  4 Core capabilities  5 Work experience  6 Education
Lead with capabilities, not history. Position for the role, not your past.

### Prompt 15: Rewrite Work Experience
Rewrite the bullet points for my most recent role.

Rules:
- Max 5 bullet points
- Each bullet: one sentence, under 20 words
- Lead with impact or scope, NOT the task
- Frame using: Reputation / Influence / Impact
- At least 2 bullets with a number; keep numbers meaningful
- No adjectives without evidence
- No soft skill bullets: lead with the outcome

Do the same for previous 2 roles within 10-12 years.

### Prompt 16: Structure Check
Check: correct order, section lengths, tech stack near top, overall length.

Senior IC/Architects: 2 pages max. Leaders: 2-3 pages.

Flag issues. Don't rewrite unless asked.

### Prompt 17: Final Alignment
Read the full resume against the JD.

For each of 3-5 critical capabilities:
- Is it visible? Where?
- Is the evidence strong enough? (10 second hiring manager test)
- Anything to amplify, reorder, cut?

Return: short list of specific changes. No full rewrite unless needed.
